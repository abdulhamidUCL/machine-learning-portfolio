# Telco Customer Churn Prediction

This project predicts whether a telecommunications company's customer is likely to leave the service (churn) based on customer demographics, subscribed services, contract type, tenure, and billing information.

The project covers data cleaning, missing-value handling, categorical encoding, feature scaling, and classification model training. I experimented with Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), Random Forest, and Support Vector Machine (SVM) to compare their performance on the churn prediction task.

**Objective:** Explore different classification algorithms and evaluate their ability to identify customers who may leave the company.
